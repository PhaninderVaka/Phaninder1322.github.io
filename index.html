Welcome, Engineers

I have over four years of hands-on experience in rocket propulsion, specializing in both solid and hybrid systems, built test rigs and various other systems. At IIT Madras, I led the development of paraffin-based hybrid rocket engines, conducting over 200 static firings and also high-pressure tests at ISRO’s SHAR rocket propulsion facilities.

As part of the ISRO-VSSC project, I was a key member of the team that engineered low-temperature gas generators for inflatable aerodynamic decelerators used in human spaceflight recovery systems. I conducted over 150 hybrid rocket lab tests as part of my M.S. thesis, focusing on eroding nozzle behavior and solid motor efficiency. This work integrated Python-based analysis, RocketCEA simulations, and data-driven techniques. I also contributed to rapid prototyping using 3D printing, and developed interactive dashboards to track propulsion performance — blending deep aerospace expertise with intelligent systems and real-time experimental validation.
